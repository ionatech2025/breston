# Breston Technologies Limited - Website Requirements Document

## Project Overview

Breston Technologies Limited is a growing provider of medical and industrial gas solutions dedicated to supporting healthcare institutions, laboratories, manufacturing industries, construction, fabrication, mining, oil and gas, food processing, electronics, and other critical sectors.

The company specializes in the supply and distribution of industrial, medical, and specialty gases together with high-quality gas accessories, equipment, and engineering solutions. Services include gas supply, cylinder distribution, gas handling equipment, medical gas pipeline systems, regulators, safety products, and technical support solutions tailored to customer needs.
